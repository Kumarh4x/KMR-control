Wireless Debugging Troubleshooting Guide
1. "Connection Refused" after rebooting or reconnecting to Wi-Fi

On Android 11+, the connection port is assigned dynamically whenever Wireless Debugging toggles or Wi-Fi reconnects (e.g. changing from port 38421 to 41295).
Solution: Open Settings > Developer Options > Wireless Debugging on your phone to check the new port, then either use Direct Connect or update the port on your saved device. You do not need to pair again!

2. "Device Unauthorized" or prompt not appearing

If your phone displays "Unauthorized", unlock your phone screen and look for the prompt titled "Allow wireless debugging on this network?" and check "Always allow from this network".
If prompt does not appear: In Developer Options, tap "Revoke USB debugging authorizations", toggle Wireless Debugging off and on, and re-connect.

3. "Connection timed out" / PC cannot reach phone IP

• Ensure your PC and phone are connected to the same Wi-Fi router / SSID.
• Check if your router has "AP Isolation" or "Client Isolation" enabled (common on public/guest Wi-Fi), which blocks devices from communicating with each other.
• Ensure your phone screen is on; some phones disable Wi-Fi radio when deep sleeping unless charging.
